name: factory-lite
description: Lightweight chain for bug fixes and small changes. Use for fix, tweak, small change, or when the user says "lite".
---
1. factory:codebase-researcher on the area. Show the map and a 3–5 line plan. ASK HUMAN: go?
2. The builder that owns the layer (django, react, infra or pipeline). For bugs, write the failing test first.
3. In parallel: factory:implementation-validator (against the plan) and factory:security-reviewer.
4. Fix Critical findings once, then show results and offer to open a PR written with the pr-format skill.
